    We first show the scheme’s correctness. Clearly, Step 1 correctly retrieves
m1 = m mod n as in Scheme 1. Step 2 is actually an unblinding phase which
is necessary to recover mn2 mod n. Step 3 is an RSA decryption with a public
exponent e = n. The final step recombines2 the original message m. The fact that
Scheme 2 is a permutation comes from the bijectivity of Eg . Again, trapdoorness
is based on the factorisation of n. Regarding one-wayness, we state :

Theorem 16. Scheme 2 is one-way if and only if RSA [n, n] is hard.
